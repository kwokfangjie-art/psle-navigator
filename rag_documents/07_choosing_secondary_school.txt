Title: Choosing a Suitable Secondary School
Source: Ministry of Education Singapore
Source type: Official government information
Last reviewed: August 2026

--- CONTENT ---

Choosing a secondary school should involve more than comparing PSLE Scores and historical Cut-Off Points.

Parents and students should consider the student's strengths, interests, abilities, learning needs and preferences when exploring secondary schools.

STRENGTHS AND INTERESTS

Students should consider schools that provide opportunities to develop their individual strengths and interests.

Relevant considerations may include:

- subjects offered;
- school programmes;
- Co-Curricular Activities (CCAs);
- Applied Learning Programmes;
- Learning for Life Programmes;
- academic interests;
- sports;
- performing arts;
- technology-related interests;
- leadership opportunities; and
- other developmental opportunities.

SCHOOL CULTURE AND ENVIRONMENT

School culture, ethos and learning environment are important considerations.

Students may perform and develop better in an environment where they:

- feel a sense of belonging;
- are supported by teachers and peers;
- can pursue their interests;
- have opportunities to contribute;
- learn at an appropriate pace; and
- feel comfortable participating in school life.

LOCATION AND TRANSPORT

Location and travelling time should also be considered.

A school that is reasonably accessible may reduce travelling time and make it easier for students to participate in CCAs, enrichment activities and other school programmes.

PSLE SCORE AND HISTORICAL CUT-OFF POINTS

Historical school Cut-Off Points can help students and parents understand previous admission outcomes.

However, historical Cut-Off Points are reference points only.

A school's Cut-Off Point can change from year to year depending on factors such as:

- the PSLE results of that year's cohort;
- students' school choices; and
- the number of available places.

A historical Cut-Off Point therefore does not guarantee admission in a future Secondary 1 Posting Exercise.

RANGE OF SCHOOL CHOICES

Students should consider a range of suitable schools rather than concentrating only on schools with historical Cut-Off Points that are very close to their PSLE Score.

MOE advises students to use all six secondary school choices and to consider schools with different Cut-Off Points.

The objective should be to identify schools that are suitable for the student's overall development rather than simply selecting the schools with the most competitive historical Cut-Off Points.

CHOICE ORDER

Students should rank schools according to their genuine preferences.

Choice order matters in Secondary 1 Posting because it can be used as a tie-breaker when students are otherwise tied under the applicable posting rules.

SCHOOL RESEARCH

Students and parents can research schools using resources such as MOE SchoolFinder.

SchoolFinder allows users to explore information including:

- school location;
- subjects;
- programmes;
- CCAs; and
- other school information.

Parents and students may also attend school open houses and review individual school websites to better understand each school's environment and opportunities.

MAKING THE DECISION TOGETHER

Parents should involve their child in discussions about secondary school choices.

The student's own interests, preferences and feelings about the school environment should form part of the decision.

There is no single school that is automatically the best choice for every student.

A suitable school is one that can support the individual student's learning needs, strengths, interests and overall development.

IMPORTANT

School programmes, admission arrangements and Cut-Off Points may change.

Students and parents should verify current information using official Ministry of Education and school sources before submitting their Secondary 1 school choices.
