Title: Full Subject-Based Banding and Posting Groups
Source: Ministry of Education Singapore
Source type: Official government information
Last reviewed: August 2026

--- CONTENT ---

Full Subject-Based Banding (Full SBB) has been fully implemented in secondary schools since 2024.

Under Full SBB, the previous Express, Normal (Academic) and Normal (Technical) stream labels have been phased out.

POSTING GROUPS

Posting Groups 1, 2 and 3 are used to facilitate students' admission into secondary schools.

Posting Groups are primarily an admissions mechanism. They should not be understood as permanent academic streams.

Once students enter secondary school, their learning experience can be customised according to their strengths, interests and learning needs.

SUBJECT LEVELS

Under Full SBB, subjects can be offered at three subject levels:

- G1
- G2
- G3

"G" stands for General.

Students may take a combination of subjects at different subject levels rather than having all of their subjects determined by a single academic stream.

A student's initial subject levels are guided by their PSLE performance and Posting Group.

Students may subsequently have opportunities to adjust the levels at which they take subjects according to their strengths, interests, learning needs and school-based considerations.

MIXED FORM CLASSES

Under Full SBB, students from different Posting Groups can be placed together in mixed form classes.

The intention is to provide students with a common secondary school experience while allowing greater flexibility in the academic levels at which individual subjects are taken.

POSTING GROUPS ARE NOT STREAMS

Posting Group 1, Posting Group 2 and Posting Group 3 should not simply be described as replacements for the old Normal (Technical), Normal (Academic) and Express streams.

Unlike the previous streaming system, Posting Groups are used to facilitate entry into secondary school and guide students' initial subject levels.

Students can subsequently take different subjects at different G1, G2 and G3 levels according to their strengths and learning needs.

SECONDARY EDUCATION CERTIFICATE

From 2027, the Singapore-Cambridge Secondary Education Certificate (SEC) examination will replace the previous N-Level and O-Level examinations.

Students will sit for subjects at their respective G1, G2 or G3 subject levels.

IMPORTANT

Full SBB policies, subject-level arrangements and post-secondary admission requirements may change over time.

Students and parents should verify current arrangements with the Ministry of Education and the relevant secondary school.
