Title: Secondary 1 Posting
Source: Ministry of Education Singapore
Source type: Official government information
Last reviewed: August 2026

--- CONTENT ---

The Secondary 1 Posting Exercise is the main process through which Primary 6 students are posted to secondary schools after receiving their PSLE results.

Students and parents should use the school-choice period to consider suitable secondary schools based on the student's PSLE score, interests, programmes, CCAs, school environment and other relevant considerations.

Historical cut-off points (COPs) should be treated only as reference points.

A school's cut-off point reflects the score of the last student admitted to that school in a previous posting exercise. The current year's cut-off point is only known after that year's Secondary 1 Posting Exercise has ended.

Because cut-off points can change from year to year, a previous year's COP does not guarantee admission in a future exercise.

When choosing schools, parents and students should not rely only on historical COPs. They should also consider whether the school's programmes, learning environment, CCAs and other opportunities are suitable for the student.

School choice order may be relevant during the posting process when students are otherwise tied under the applicable posting rules. Users should therefore list their school choices carefully and in genuine order of preference.

Posting arrangements and tie-break rules may change over time. Users should always verify the latest Secondary 1 Posting procedures, dates and rules with the Ministry of Education.
