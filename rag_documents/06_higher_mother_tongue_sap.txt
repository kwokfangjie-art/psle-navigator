Title: Higher Mother Tongue Language and SAP Schools
Source: Ministry of Education Singapore
Source type: Official government information
Last reviewed: August 2026

--- CONTENT ---

HIGHER MOTHER TONGUE LANGUAGE

Higher Mother Tongue Language (HMTL) provides students who demonstrate strong ability and interest in their Mother Tongue Language with an opportunity to study the language at a higher level.

HMTL includes:

- Higher Chinese Language
- Higher Malay Language
- Higher Tamil Language

2026 SECONDARY 1 ELIGIBILITY

From the 2026 Secondary 1 cohort, students who perform well in their Mother Tongue Language at PSLE can offer Higher Mother Tongue Language in secondary school regardless of their overall PSLE Score.

Students who obtain either:

- AL1 or AL2 in Mother Tongue Language; or
- Distinction or Merit in Higher Mother Tongue Language

at PSLE will be able to offer HMTL in secondary school regardless of their overall PSLE Score.

This represents a change from the previous eligibility criteria, which also considered the student's overall PSLE Score.

The change is intended to better recognise students' strengths and interests in Mother Tongue Language and provide more students with opportunities to develop their language proficiency.

Students and parents should check the latest MOE information and the relevant secondary school's subject offerings when considering Higher Mother Tongue Language.

SPECIAL ASSISTANCE PLAN (SAP) SCHOOLS

Special Assistance Plan schools provide an environment that places particular emphasis on developing students' proficiency and appreciation of the Chinese language and culture while maintaining a strong bilingual education.

Students considering a SAP school should look beyond historical cut-off points and consider whether the school's language and cultural environment is suitable for their interests and learning needs.

HIGHER CHINESE AND SAP POSTING

Higher Chinese Language performance can be relevant to admission to SAP schools.

Under the PSLE scoring and Secondary 1 posting framework, students who take Higher Chinese Language may receive a posting advantage for admission to SAP schools.

Where students with the same PSLE Score are competing for limited places in the same SAP school, Higher Chinese Language grades may be considered before the normal Secondary 1 posting tie-breakers.

Students and parents should refer to the latest MOE Secondary 1 Posting guidance for the current rules.

SCHOOL-SPECIFIC INFORMATION

Not every secondary school offers the same Mother Tongue Language options or programmes.

Users should check the relevant school's current information to confirm:

- whether Higher Mother Tongue Language is offered;
- which Higher Mother Tongue Languages are available;
- school-specific subject arrangements; and
- other relevant language programmes.

IMPORTANT

Higher Mother Tongue Language eligibility and Secondary 1 posting policies can change over time.

The information in this knowledge base is intended for educational guidance.

Users should verify current eligibility requirements and school-specific offerings with the Ministry of Education and the relevant secondary school.
