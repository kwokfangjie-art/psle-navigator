Title: Integrated Programme (IP)
Source: Ministry of Education Singapore
Source type: Official government information
Last reviewed: August 2026

--- CONTENT ---

The Integrated Programme (IP) is a six-year educational programme offered by selected schools in Singapore.

It is intended for academically strong students who can benefit from broader learning experiences in both academic and non-academic areas.

SIX-YEAR PROGRAMME

Students in the Integrated Programme follow a six-year pathway covering secondary and pre-university education.

One key feature of the Integrated Programme is that students do not take the conventional O-Level examinations at the end of Secondary 4.

The time freed from preparing for the O-Level examinations allows participating schools to provide broader and deeper learning experiences.

LEARNING EXPERIENCE

The Integrated Programme may suit students who are comfortable with a more independent learning environment and who can cope with the academic demands of the programme.

IP schools may provide opportunities for:

- broader academic learning;
- deeper exploration of selected subjects;
- interdisciplinary learning;
- research and project work;
- leadership development;
- enrichment activities; and
- non-academic development.

The specific curriculum and programmes differ between schools.

QUALIFICATIONS

At the end of the six-year Integrated Programme, students obtain a qualification depending on the school and programme they attend.

These may include:

- Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level qualification;
- International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma; or
- NUS High School Diploma.

The qualification offered therefore depends on the particular IP school or pathway.

CHOOSING AN IP SCHOOL

Students and parents should not choose an Integrated Programme school based only on historical PSLE cut-off points.

They should also consider:

- the student's learning style;
- academic readiness;
- interests and strengths;
- the school's curriculum and programmes;
- school culture and environment;
- CCAs and enrichment opportunities;
- location and travelling time; and
- the eventual pre-university pathway and qualification.

IP INDICATOR

A school being identified as an IP school means that it offers an Integrated Programme pathway.

It does not mean that every student in that school necessarily follows the same programme or admission pathway.

Users should check the individual school's current information for the specific programmes and admission routes available.

IMPORTANT

Programme structures and admission arrangements may change.

Students and parents should verify current Integrated Programme information with the Ministry of Education and the relevant school.
